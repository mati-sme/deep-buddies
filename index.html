How to play

Move & dig: touch anywhere and drag (an invisible joystick follows your finger), or use Arrow keys / WASD. Tap a block to walk over and dig it.

Pip explores on his own: he digs, sniffs out treasure and fights cave creatures near him. Tap the paw button, then a spot, to send him there.

Your lantern runs out of oil. Grab oil flasks, dig deep and collect ore. You sell it at camp.

Spend coins on the Upgrade Map. At 100 m you can Cave-in for Relics and evolve Pip on the Relic Map.

Watch out for green gas rocks, water and lava.
